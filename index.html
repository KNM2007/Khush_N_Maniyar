HOW TO PLAY

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Destroy all enemy rooms on the map. Each room contains one enemy. Clear every room to win!

🕹️ CONTROLS

W A S D — Move your player

Mouse — Aim

Left Click — Shoot

💡 TIPS

• Enter a room to activate its enemy — they only shoot when you're inside!

• Bullets bounce off walls — use angles to your advantage.

• Collect medkits ➕ outside rooms for +10 HP (spawns every 15s).

• Clear a full column of rooms for a column clear bonus.

👥 MULTIPLAYER

P1: WASD + Mouse click to shoot

P2: Arrow keys + Spacebar to shoot

Most enemies killed wins! Bullets don't hurt teammates.
